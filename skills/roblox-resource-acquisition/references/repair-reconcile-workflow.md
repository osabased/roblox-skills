- **Iterative proof, test, or generated-child repair:** use [repair-loop.md](repair-loop.md) for classification, one-fix cycles, regression reruns, budget, convergence, escalation, and learning emission.
- **Upstream identity, source facts, qualification, or trust invalidated by the defect:** use [qualification-workflow.md](qualification-workflow.md).
- **Generated-child structural, behavioral, bundle, or catalog validation invalidated by the defect:** use [generation-validation.md](generation-validation.md).
- **Trust/verification status, proof-target state, persistent records, or final reporting:** use [state-policy.md](state-policy.md).

The parent [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) selects among these branches directly; this router preserves compatibility without duplicating their rules.
